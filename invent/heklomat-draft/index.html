HEKLOMAT-1

A ~5 000 NOK machine that crochets the eight MASKLAB bucket hats — magic ring to brim — using a stitch-presentation wheel so the hook never has to hunt for the last round’s loops. This folder is a standalone evaluation kit: patent, IKEA-style build, printable STLs, and a digital twin driven by the same Maskekode stream as the hats.

Served only from the concept static server (http://127.0.0.1:8899/invent/heklomat-draft/). It is not on masklab.vercel.app and does not share the Vite app on port 5173.

Patent application

English international-style filing: field, prior art, 7 figures, 18 claims. The money figure is the presentation wheel’s pick-up / present / release zones.

IKEA-style build guide

Fifteen assembly steps, calibration, first-hat procedure, and a complete purchased BOM in NOK (~5 050–5 320).

3D-printable parts

Realtime previs

Why this is feasible

Crochet is the craft machines supposedly cannot do. Two research machines show where it actually fails: Croche-Matic (Harvard GSD, ICRA 2023) can chain, single crochet, increase and decrease in the round, but only at ~50% stitch success — it cannot reliably find and hold open the previous round’s loops. CroMat (HSBI / TU Dresden) solved holding: a row of latch needles keeps the last row open, but only for flat fabric.

HEKLOMAT-1 bends CroMat’s latch row into a circle and keeps Croche-Matic’s radial spiral, via a mechanism neither has: an eight-tooth stitch-presentation wheel that, like a film sprocket, picks up the previous round, presents one loop held open at a fixed work point, and releases it behind the hook. Increases are two hook cycles on one tooth. Diameter can grow from 10 to 144 stitches without searching.

All eight hats in the app are 100% fastmaske / single crochet, top-down spiral, tapestry colourwork, no decreases. The machine only needs MR, FM, INC, COL, SLST, FO — the Maskekode already compiled from each pattern.
