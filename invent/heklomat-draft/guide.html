Calibration

  1. C-axis zero. Jog until a mandrel bayonet faces +X. Save as C=0. One stitch must equal 360° / current round count.
  2. Wheel mesh depth. A latch tooth should lift the previous round’s V without dragging fabric off the mandrel. Start 1.5 mm outside the stitch radius; inch in 0.2 mm until the loop seats in the latch.
  3. Hook plunge. At the bottom of (b), the hook tip is 2–3 mm through the presented loop, not through the mandrel. Set a Z min software limit.
  4. 10-chain test. Work ten chains then ten fastmasker in a ring on the crown. Expect ≥9/10 successful presentations before loading a hat program.

First hat

Load NORGE training (smallest colour-run stress after the crown). Magic-ring setup is 30 s automated. Unity speed is ~8 s/stitch; do not leave the first hat unattended. Expect about 10 hours including pauses. On FINISHED: slip-stitch, fasten off, lift the mandrel, steam the brim.
