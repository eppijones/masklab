Four directions where the red-white-blue reads at eye level — from a group photo, across a stadium, in a selfie. Red carries the identity so it never reads as Iceland. Each is buildable in ~8 h with your doubled Bomull, red Smart and kobolt Petunia on a 6 mm hook. Point to one (or mix), and I'll build the full lookbook + construction chart for it.

1A Front-visible

Korsbåndet

A red hat carries the flag. A white-and-blue stripe wraps the whole band, and a short cross-bar sits dead front — the Scandinavian cross "unrolled" around your head, unmistakably Norwegian from any seat.

Build: body in red (Smart doubled) or white with red band — your call. Stripes are 2 plain rounds. No tapestry.

1B Closest to your photo

Stripa

Your reference photo, dialled up. A quiet white bøttehatt with one bold red-white-blue band circling the lower crown. Calm, premium, obviously Norwegian — and the fastest possible flag statement.

Build: the easiest of the four — three solid colour rounds, no colorwork at all. ~6 h, lots of buffer.

1C Boldest

Frontkorset

One big flag badge square on the front panel — a full red field with the white-bordered blue cross. Reads as a flag instantly, close up or across a crowd. The loudest supporter statement.

Build: worked on the flat front only, so surface slip-stitch stays small and rippable. Slightly more colorwork than the others.

1D Most "crochet"

Bølgen

A white hat with a red-and-blue chevron wave riding the whole brim — the coastline, "rowed home." Softer and more textural than a flag, but the tricolour still reads clearly all the way around.

Build: the wave is just sc–hdc–dc repeats in colour rounds — no charts, very forgiving of imperfection.
